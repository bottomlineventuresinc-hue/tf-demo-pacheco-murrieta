Scope of work

What we will not do

  • Lay the first course before the line and the level are set.
  • Quote masonry from photos alone.
  • Hide material costs inside a vague line item.
  • Dig before underground utilities are marked.
  • Leave debris or leftover material in the yard.

The paperwork

What the written quote covers.

One page, line by line, before any work starts. If something is not on the list, it is named in the exclusions on the same page.

  1. 01The work, described in plain words
  2. 02Length, height, and layout, measured
  3. 03Materials named by type, size, and color
  4. 04Footing or base spelled out
  5. 05Removal and haul-away of old masonry, or a note that it is excluded
  6. 06Permit costs when the job needs one
  7. 07Anything that could change the price, flagged up front
  8. 08What is not included, in writing
